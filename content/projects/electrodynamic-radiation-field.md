**What:** A Python simulation of the radiation field produced by an
accelerating point charge, accounting for retarded time (the finite
speed of light between the charge and the observation point). Modeled
two cases: a charge in circular orbit and a charge oscillating linearly
along an axis, producing circular and linear polarization respectively.

**Why:** Radiation fields fall off as 1/r rather than 1/r² like static
fields — wanted to actually see that far-field behavior rather than just
work through the equation on paper.

**Result:** Two rendered animations (circular and linear polarization)
showing the field's angular dependence and propagation.
