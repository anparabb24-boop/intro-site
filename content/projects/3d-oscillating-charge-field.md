**What:** A Python animation (Matplotlib) visualizing a decaying wave
surface — modeling the field behavior of a charge oscillating about an
axis, with the decay exaggerated for visual clarity.

**Why:** Wanted to build something closer to the 3Blue1Brown style of
math visualization — animated, camera-directed, and physically motivated
rather than just a static plot.

**Result:** A cylindrical-to-Cartesian coordinate mapping driving a
real-time animated surface, with a scripted camera path that rotates
around the field to show the full peak-and-valley structure.
